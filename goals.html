Mission · working toward

The 10X Recovery Initiative — the target, not the present tense.

Mission statement (goal)

Help people move from surviving to running a written day: paid rent, a skill, a schedule, and a reason not to drift. Pair that with a quiet house that has rules, a live-in manager, and no clinic on the property.

The long target is a funded, durable operation that teaches work, money, and systems while someone has a bed. That is the aim. It is not the current P&L.

Problem we are aiming at

Getting through the first stretch of sobriety is not the whole job. People slide when there is no purpose, no cash, and no next skill. Traditional treatment, when someone already has it elsewhere, often stops at the clinical piece and leaves the money and housing piece empty. That gap is what this company is trying to fill with education, house rules, and work practice — not by pretending a grant already landed.

What we are working toward

  1. Foundation. One Los Angeles building, about 15 to 30 units. Owner keeps title. Master lease or owner-carry. Rent above market. Live-in manager.
  2. House operations. Written rules, rent day, quiet hours. Housing only on site. No detox, counseling, or treatment in the building.
  3. Education lane. Books, sales practice, money habits, AI tools on a laptop — the library and the Front Office product.
  4. Later funding lane. If a separate nonprofit is formed and funded, that entity can apply for public or foundation money under its own name and rules. That entity does not exist as an awarded grantee today. Do not treat this page as a submitted proposal or a notice of award.
