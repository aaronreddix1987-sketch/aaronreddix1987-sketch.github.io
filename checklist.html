The Missed-Call Checklist

Eight quick checks you can do today, with your own phone, for free. Then do the math at the bottom.

Save this page. A copy of the link is also emailed to you.

Eight checks

Do these in order

Count one week

Open the call log on your phone. Count every missed call from the last 7 days that you did not call back. Write the number down.

Call yourself

From another phone, call your business at lunch and again after hours. Listen to what a customer hears.

Fix the greeting

Your voicemail should say your business name, when you call back, and ask for a name, number and what they need.

Empty the mailbox

A full mailbox turns callers away. Clear it out today.

Check your listing

Search your business on Google. Make sure the phone number and hours are right.

Set call-back times

Pick two times a day to return calls, like noon and 5 PM. Put them in your phone as alarms.

If you choose to text back yourself

Our pilot does not send texts. If you want to, send a quick text by hand: “Sorry I missed you. This is [name] at [business]. How can I help?”

Ask how they found you

On every call back, ask where they found your number. Do more of what works.
