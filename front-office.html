Why it works

Buy back your time, starting with the phone

In his book Buy Back Your Time, Dan Martell teaches owners a simple loop: audit where your time goes, transfer the low-value work, and fill the freed hours with work that pays. We apply that loop to your front desk.

Audit

On the discovery call we map how calls and leads reach you today, and count where they get lost.

Transfer

Answering missed calls and taking messages move off your plate and onto the system.

Fill

You spend the freed hours on the paid work and the customers already in front of you.

The DRIP Matrix

Where front-desk work belongs

Martell sorts every task by two questions: does it make money, and does it give you energy?

D

Delegation

Low money, drains you. Returning missed calls and writing down messages. This moves to the system first.

R

Replacement

Makes money, drains you. Chasing quotes and follow-ups. Scheduled follow-up takes this over.

I

Investment

Low money, gives you energy. Learning, health, family. The hours you get back protect this.

P

Production

High money, gives you energy. Doing the job and closing the sale. This is where you belong.

Your Buyback Rate

What is an hour of your time worth?

Martell's rule: take what you pay yourself in a year, divide by 2,000 working hours, then divide by 4. Any task you can hand off for less than that number should not be yours.
