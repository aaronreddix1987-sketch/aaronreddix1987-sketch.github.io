The DRIP Matrix

Where front-desk work belongs

Martell sorts every task by two questions: does it make money, and does it give you energy?

D

Delegation

Low money, drains you. Returning missed calls and writing down messages. This moves to the system first.

R

Replacement

Makes money, drains you. Chasing quotes and call-backs. The AI answers first and emails you who to call back.

I

Investment

Low money, gives you energy. Learning, health, family. The hours you get back protect this.

P

Production

High money, gives you energy. Doing the job and closing the sale. This is where you belong.

Your Buyback Rate

What is an hour of your time worth?

Martell's rule: take what you pay yourself in a year, divide by 2,000 working hours, then divide by 4. Any task you can hand off for less than that number should not be yours.
