City tax & deadlines

LA Office of Finance Counter Closure Nov 25 to Dec 4 for MyTaxLA

The City of Los Angeles Office of Finance will close its public counters, including the Van Nuys branch at 6262 Van Nuys Blvd, Suite 110, along with online registration from Wednesday, November 25 through Friday, December 4, 2026 while switching to its new MyTaxLA portal. MyTaxLA launches Monday, December 7, 2026 for upcoming annual business tax renewals, while urgent registrations before December 7 can be submitted by email form. Owners should also mark December 31 for annual Police, Fire, Alarm, and Tobacco permit renewals, and February 28 for timely annual business tax filings required to claim the $100,000 Small Business Exemption.

Source: City of Los Angeles Office of Finance

Valley grants

LA Metro Business Interruption Grants Open Along Van Nuys Blvd

LA Metro is accepting rolling Business Interruption Fund grant applications from small mom-and-pop shops directly affected by East San Fernando Valley Light Rail construction on Van Nuys Boulevard. Qualifying corridor businesses with 25 or fewer full-time employees and documented revenue loss can apply for Standard Grants up to $60,000 per impact year for rent, payroll, utilities, or insurance, while eligible microbusinesses and street vendors can seek streamlined awards up to $1,500. Applications and corridor workshops are administered with Pacific Coast Regional Business Finance through LA Metro.

Source: LA Metro Business Interruption Fund

Check eligibility at metro.net →
Permits & loan help

Free Permit & Loan Packaging at Three Valley BusinessSource Centers

Los Angeles business owners and resident startups can book free one-on-one consulting, tax preparation guidance, and loan-application packaging through the City’s BusinessSource Centers. Bilingual advisors help owners complete City of Los Angeles, LA County, and State of California permit paperwork, including applications for the City Sidewalk and Park Vendor Permit Program. Three San Fernando Valley locations serve local owners in Canoga Park (6800 Owensmouth Ave, Suite 150), Van Nuys (5805 Sepulveda Blvd, Suite 801), and Pacoima (13168 Van Nuys Blvd).

Source: City of LA Economic & Workforce Development Department

Find Valley centers at laewdd.org →

City wage & contract rules

Check LA City $18.42 Minimum Wage Posters & Freelance Contracts

Employers with staff working at least two hours in a week inside the City of Los Angeles must pay at least the $18.42 hourly minimum wage effective July 1, 2026 and provide at least 48 hours of annual paid sick leave. Workplaces must display the current Office of Wage Standards notice in English and any language spoken by 5 percent or more of on-site staff, and retain payroll records for four years. Businesses hiring independent contractors for professional services of $250 or more must also use written contracts stating scope, pay, and payment dates under California’s Freelance Worker Protection Act.

Source: City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards
