Good to know

Free help comes first

  • Credit reports are free at AnnualCreditReport.com, and disputing errors with Equifax, Experian and TransUnion is free.
  • Agency complaints are free with the CFPB, FTC, your state Attorney General and county consumer affairs.
  • Court self-help centers are free at selfhelp.courts.ca.gov and in every LA courthouse.
  • Legal aid may be available for free at LawHelpCA.org.
