What TrenchAI covers

Tax liens and tax-deed auctions

How unpaid property taxes lead to lien certificates in some states and tax-deed or tax-defaulted property auctions in others. In California, counties such as Los Angeles hold tax-defaulted property sales and do not sell lien certificates, according to the LA County Property Tax Portal. Rules, deposits and redemption periods differ by jurisdiction, so read the official local sale rules.

Foreclosures

How lender foreclosures, trustee sales and sheriff sales work, what “as is” really means, and why title, liens, occupancy and condition must be checked with a title company and qualified professionals before bidding.

Owner financing

How seller carry-back, lease-options and master leases work, which questions to ask an owner or agent, and why every deal needs its own legal, tax and title review.
