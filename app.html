iPhone steps Android steps

On an iPhone, follow the three steps below. On Android, an Install button appears here in Chrome.

iPhone and iPad

Install with Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
Android

Install with Chrome

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app at the top of this page, or tap the three dots in the corner.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.
