Session 1 · Week of November 23, 2026

Topic: Start where you are

Listen (2 min 40 s, AI voice):

1. Settle in

Find a quiet spot. Take a slow breath in, hold it for a moment, and let it go. Do that three times.

2. Reading (written by Total Transformation Inc.)

You do not have to fix your whole life today. You only have to take the next right step from the place you are standing right now. Some of us wait until we feel ready, until the money is right, until the shame is gone, until everyone forgives us. That day may not come on our schedule.

Recovery and rebuilding start in the middle of the mess, not after it. Where you are is not where you have to stay. But it is the only place you can start from. So name it honestly: what is working, what is not, and the one small thing you can do this week. A phone call. A meeting. A walk. A glass of water and a full night of sleep. Small steps, repeated, become a new life.

3. Reflection questions

Pause after each one. Write your answers down, or share them with someone you trust.

  1. If you were honest with yourself, where are you right now, today?
  2. What is one thing that is already going right, even if it is small?
  3. What has been stopping you from starting, and is it really true?
  4. Who is one safe person you can reach out to this week?

4. Gratitude and commitment

  • Gratitude: name three things you are grateful for today. Keep them simple: a roof, a friend, another day.
  • Commitment: finish this sentence out loud or on paper: "This week, I will take one small step by..." Keep it small enough that you will actually do it.

5. Close

Thank you for showing up for yourself today. If you want more support this week, join one of the free meetings below. A new session goes up every Sunday evening.

Using this with a group

You can read this session together with friends or housemates. Keep it simple: one person reads, everyone may share or pass, no cross-talk or advice-giving, what is shared stays in the room. If anyone is in danger or talks about harming themselves, stop and call 988 or 911.
