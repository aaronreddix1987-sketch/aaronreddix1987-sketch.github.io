Love and Rehab. The reality show package. Created by Aaron T. Reddix, Total Transformation Incorporated.

Every recovery professional knows the unofficial rule: no new relationships in the first year. And every recovery professional knows the unofficial truth: it happens anyway. Because people who are learning to feel again feel everything, at maximum volume.

Set inside a real, structured sober living home in Los Angeles, Love and Rehab follows six adults through 90 days of rebuilding their lives. The jobs, the step work, the house meetings, the drug tests, the curfews. And the forbidden current of attraction running underneath all of it.

The executive, who traded a corner office for a chore chart. The young mother, 90 days from the custody hearing that decides everything. The charmer, whose charisma was always his first addiction. The quiet one, carrying the season's heaviest secret.

Every romantic choice is also a survival choice. Is this love, or is this relapse wearing love's clothes?

A founder from South Los Angeles who lived this world from both sides of the intake desk. Ten episodes. Real stakes. A comeback story the camera has never told honestly, until now.

Your recovery comes first. Your heart didn't get the memo.

This is a creative project. It is not for sale right now.
