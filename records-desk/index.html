Evidence checklist

Use this list to organize your own files. Do not share records or personal information on this preview.

For credit-report errors, see the CFPB’s credit report dispute guide.

  • The record in question

    A copy with the relevant item marked.

  • A short timeline

    Relevant dates, what happened, and when you noticed the discrepancy.

  • Supporting documents

    Copies of statements, notices, receipts, or correspondence. Keep originals for your records.

  • A communication log

    Any prior submissions, receipts, replies, and confirmation numbers, kept in your own files.
