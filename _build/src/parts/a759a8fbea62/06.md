# Appendix B: Discovery Call Script

Use this as a guide, not a script to read aloud. Adapt the wording to your own voice.

**Opening (1 minute).** "Thanks for making time. I'd suggest we spend about 30 minutes: I'll ask some questions about the business and what you're hoping to change, you can ask me anything, and at the end we'll decide together whether a next step makes sense. If it doesn't look like a fit, I'll tell you, and I'll try to point you to someone better suited. Does that work?"

**Context.**

- "Tell me about the business. What do you do, and who is a typical customer?"
- "Where do most new customers come from today?"
- "What are your busy and slow periods?"

**Goal.**

- "What made you want to look at this now?"
- "If this went well, what would be different in six months?"
- "Roughly how many more of what kind of customer would make a real difference?"
- "What's a typical new customer worth to you over a year?"

**Current state.**

- "What have you tried so far? What worked, and what didn't?"
- "Who handles marketing now, and how much time does it take?"
- "Can we look at your profile or accounts together for a minute?"

**Constraints.**

- "Have you set aside a budget range for this? Our packages run from $[low] to $[high] a month."
- "Who else is involved in a decision like this?"
- "Is there anything that would stop you moving ahead, even if the proposal looked right?"

**Close.**

- If fit: "Based on what you've said, I think the [option] option is the best match. I'll send a short proposal with three options by [date]. Could we book 15 minutes on [date] to go through it?"
- If no fit: "Honestly, I don't think we're the right fit for [need]. You might look at [provider] instead."

# Appendix D: Service Agreement Checklist

Have your agreement template reviewed by a licensed attorney in your state. Use this checklist to confirm it covers the essentials.

:::checklist Agreement contents
- [ ] Parties, effective date, and chosen service tier.
- [ ] Scope: inclusions, exclusions, and change-request process.
- [ ] Fees, billing date, payment method, late-payment terms.
- [ ] Ad spend: billed directly to client, or deposit-and-rebill terms.
- [ ] Minimum term, notice period, and termination for cause.
- [ ] Ownership: client owns accounts, data, and paid deliverables; agency retains templates and methods.
- [ ] Access: manager access only; handover within a stated period on termination (no more than seven business days for Google Business Profiles).
- [ ] Approvals: who approves what, response times, written approval for review replies.
- [ ] Client responsibilities: information, access, timely feedback.
- [ ] Confidentiality and data handling, including AI tool use.
- [ ] Compliance: no fake reviews, accurate claims, required disclosures.
- [ ] No guarantee of results.
- [ ] Limitation of liability and indemnities.
- [ ] Governing law and dispute resolution.
- [ ] Signatures.
:::
