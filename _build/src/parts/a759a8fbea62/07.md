
# Glossary

**Activity metric.** A count of work performed, such as posts published or reviews answered. Proves work happened; does not prove results.

**Attribution.** Assigning credit for a customer or sale to a particular marketing activity.

**Buyback Rate.** Dan Martell's delegation threshold: annual pay divided by 2,000 hours, divided by four.

**Camcorder Method.** Martell's technique of recording yourself doing a task and turning the recording into a written playbook.

**CAN-SPAM Act.** US law setting rules for commercial email, including accurate headers, a postal address, and a working opt-out.

**Core Four.** Alex Hormozi's four lead channels: warm outreach, posting content, cold outreach, and paid ads.

**Discovery call.** A structured first conversation to diagnose a prospect's situation and decide whether there is a fit.

**Fixed pricing.** Charging a set fee for a defined scope, regardless of hours.

**Floor price.** The lowest price at which serving a client covers its share of costs, owner pay, and margin.

**Gross margin.** Gross profit divided by revenue.

**Leading indicator.** A metric that signals response, such as calls or clicks, before outcomes such as sales are known.

**Lifetime value (LTV).** Monthly gross profit per client multiplied by average months retained.

**Material connection.** A relationship between an endorser and a seller (payment, free product, family tie) that could affect how an endorsement is weighed; must be disclosed under FTC guidance when the audience would not expect it.

**Pain line.** Martell's term for the point at which a founder's personal capacity can no longer fuel the business.

**Playbook.** A written, step-by-step procedure for a recurring task.

**Positioning statement.** One sentence stating whom you serve, with what, toward what outcome.

**Productized service.** A service sold as a named package with fixed scope, price, and process.

**Replacement Ladder.** Martell's sequence of roles to hand off: administrative assistant, delivery or operations, marketing, sales, leadership.

**Retainer.** A fixed monthly fee for ongoing work.

**Revenue per delivery hour.** Monthly revenue divided by hours spent on client delivery.

**Scope creep.** Gradual expansion of work beyond what was agreed, without a price change.

**TCPA.** The US Telephone Consumer Protection Act, which restricts autodialed and prerecorded calls and texts without consent.

**Value Equation.** Hormozi's model: value equals dream outcome times perceived likelihood, divided by time delay times effort and sacrifice.

**Value-based pricing.** Setting a fee by reference to the outcome's worth to the client rather than the cost of delivery.
