Questions

Before you ask

How do I pay?

After the free call you get a written scope and an invoice. You pay nothing before that. Never send card numbers by text or email.

Is there a contract?

One-time jobs have a one-page scope. Monthly plans are month to month, and you can cancel any time.

Who owns the website or app?

You do. The web address, the files and the app store accounts are in your name.

Is the home-screen app a real app?

It installs from your website, gets its own icon and opens full screen like an app. It is not listed in the Apple App Store or Google Play. If you need a store listing, choose the App Store App level.

Does the AI phone agent make sales calls?

No. It answers the calls that come in to your business. The Federal Communications Commission has confirmed that calls using an AI-generated voice need the prior consent of the person called, so we do not use it for cold calls. Read the FCC ruling.

What if I only need one small thing?

Start with One Automation at $297 or the 7-Day Pilot at $97. Both are meant to be small.
