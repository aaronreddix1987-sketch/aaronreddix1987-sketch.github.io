Website intake

If you completed Stripe checkout, look for our intake email, titled "Your one-page website: send your details". Reply with your details. We’ll email your site link after we build and check it. Check your spam folder, or call (747) 301-8586 if you need help.

What to put in your reply

  • The phone number for the call button.
  • Your services, one per line (up to 12).
  • Your hours, for example "Mon to Fri 8 AM to 5 PM, Sat 9 to 1, Sun closed".
  • The cities or neighborhoods you serve.
  • Optional: two or three sentences about your business, a short tagline, a color you like.
  • Optional: up to 6 photos of your work, attached to the email. Only send photos you took or have the right to use.

Do not send card numbers, passwords or Social Security numbers. We never need them.
