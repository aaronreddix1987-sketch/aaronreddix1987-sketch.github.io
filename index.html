Built by Someone Who's Been There

Aaron Thomas Reddix founded Total Transformation Institute after experiencing incarceration, homelessness, identity theft, and the systematic barriers that prevent formerly incarcerated individuals from rebuilding their lives.

Every product in our store was created from lived experience — the record clearing toolkit, the credit dispute letters, the housing application templates, the business startup guide. These are the tools Aaron wished he had when he got out.

TTI's mission: Reentry peer support, sober living development, and workforce training for formerly incarcerated individuals in Los Angeles, California.
