What we are trying to do with one building.

Total Transformation Inc. is a California for-profit. Aaron T. Reddix lives on site as manager. We are looking for one existing Los Angeles apartment building of about 15 to 30 units. You pick the building. You keep title.

The longer mission is on the goals page. That page is the destination sketch. It is not a claim that the program is already funded or that a lease is already signed.

The ask

  • Master lease or owner-carry. No 25% down.
  • Rent above market for the structure you choose.
  • Live-in manager on site.
  • Housing and written house rules only.
  • No detox, counseling, or treatment on the property.
  • No claim that a lease is already signed or that rent is already collecting.

Why a building owner would care

Idle time and unpaid vacancies cost owners. The operating idea is simple: a tenant-operator who lives in the building, pays the rent, screens for house-rule compliance, and runs a structured daily schedule for residents who want work, skills, and a quiet house — not a clinic.

Education and AI tools stay off-site or on a laptop: books, sales practice, calendar, missed-call follow-up. That is business and education. It is not a licensed treatment program and it is not billed as one.

On the property

  • Manager on site.
  • Rent paid to the owner of record.

Not on the property

  • No Medi-Cal claim from this house.
