## Portals that actually take for-profit LA businesses

1. CalOSBA funding board — https://calosba.ca.gov/for-small-businesses-and-non-profits/funding-opportunities-for-small-businesses-and-nonprofits/
   Status: no active direct-to-business grant on the official page as of this write-up. Watch it.
2. California Grants Portal — https://www.grants.ca.gov/
   Filter: small business, Los Angeles, for-profit.
3. LA EWDD / BusinessSource — https://ewdd.lacity.org/
   City of LA intake. North Hollywood is City of LA, not unincorporated county.
4. LA County DEO — https://opportunity.lacounty.gov/
   County programs. Brick-and-mortar unincorporated rules often exclude a Case Ave home office.
5. Valley Economic Alliance / VEDC (NoHo / Valley) — search current microloan and technical-assistance intake.
6. Pacific Coast Regional SDC — SBA technical assistance, not a check in the mail.
7. Opportunity Fund / Accion Opportunity Fund — microloan, not a gift.
8. IBank Small Business Finance Center — https://www.ibank.ca.gov/
   Guarantee / loan products.
9. Kiva — 0% microloan via trustees. Not a grant.
10. LISC LA Genesis / small-business products — confirm current for-profit window before applying.
