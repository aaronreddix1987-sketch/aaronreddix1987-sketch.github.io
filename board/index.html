01 BUILD & SYSTEMS

Hourly checks

Public pages and selected workflow evidence, not blanket agent health.

    How checks work

    Public-page checks look for a successful response and expected content. System and skills checks only cover the specified run or files. None of these checks places a call, tests SMS, charges a card or confirms delivery. Reports older than three hours are not treated as current.

    02 SALES EVIDENCE

    From interest to delivery

    Totals and order records stay on the private sales whiteboard. Nothing here implies zero orders.

    1. 01
      Inquiry received

      An inbound message or a permissioned conversation was logged.

    2. 02
      Qualified

      The buyer’s need, fit and next step were confirmed.

    3. 03
      Payment verified

      A live payment succeeded. Visiting checkout does not count.

    4. 04
      Delivery verified

      Product receipt or pilot activation was separately confirmed.

    Release rule: Don’t call a pilot ready based on a checkout or a configured agent alone.
