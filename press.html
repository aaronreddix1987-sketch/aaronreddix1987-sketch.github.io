LA founder launches AI front desk so small shops stop losing missed calls.

Total Transformation Inc. opens a $97, 7-day pilot for Los Angeles trades and home-service businesses.

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 23, 2026 - Total Transformation Inc. (TTI), a new Los Angeles company founded by Aaron T. Reddix, today opened a 7-day pilot of its AI Front Desk, a phone assistant built for small businesses that miss calls while the owner is on a job.

When a business misses a call, the AI Front Desk answers in English, asks for the caller's name, phone number and what they need, and emails that summary to the owner. It does not send texts, book appointments, take payments or call customers back; the owner decides who to call.

"Plumbers, mechanics, tow drivers and stylists can't pick up the phone with their hands full," said Reddix. "I wanted something simple that makes sure a missed call isn't a lost call, and I wanted to be honest about what it does and doesn't do."

Reddix grew up in South Central Los Angeles and rebuilt his life through recovery, work and new skills before starting TTI. The company uses AI agents for much of its day-to-day work and says so openly: its public phone assistant identifies as an AI when asked, and its free online workshop uses a labeled AI-generated presenter.

TTI is pre-revenue and has no paying clients yet. The pilot costs $97 for seven days for one business. Owners who want to keep the service after the pilot pay $297 a month, month to month, with no contract.

For owners who want to measure the problem first, TTI sells a $9 Lead Leak Calculator & Missed-Call Tracker that estimates missed-call costs from the owner's own numbers. TTI also offers tools for real estate agents, including The Agent's AI Prompt Desk ($17).

Reporters and business owners can hear the AI phone assistant by calling (747) 301-8586.
