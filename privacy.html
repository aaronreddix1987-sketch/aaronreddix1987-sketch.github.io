Privacy

How we handle your information.

Updated November 22, 2026 (visit counts and text messages added).

Total Transformation Inc. (TTI) is a for-profit California corporation in Los Angeles. This page says what information we get and what we do with it.

Payments

Stripe runs every checkout. Stripe collects your name, email, card and billing details. We never see or keep your full card number. Stripe has its own privacy policy.

From Stripe we get your name, email, what you bought and the amount paid. We use it to deliver your order and help if something goes wrong.

Phone calls

Calls to our AI phone lines, including (747) 301-8586 and pilot lines we set up for businesses, are answered by an AI, not a person.

The AI asks for the caller’s name, number and what they need. A written summary is emailed to the business owner who runs that line. Our phone provider processes the call so the AI can answer it.

Email

Emails to TTI are read and answered with help from an AI assistant.

Downloads

Guides and workbooks are delivered on a download page after checkout. Anyone with the link can open that page, so keep your link to yourself.

Website orders

If you buy a one-page website, we use the details you send us (services, hours, photos, phone) to build your page. Your page is public once it is live.

Visit counts

This website counts visits with a small script that keeps only anonymous totals: which page was opened, which site or post the visitor came from (the utm_source in the link), and clicks on buy and call buttons. It sets no cookies and TTI stores no IP address, name or device ID. The totals are kept by Abacus, a free counting service, which sees the request like any web server does. To turn counting off in your browser, open any page with ?tti_nocount=1 added to the link.

Text messages (SMS)

TTI does not send text messages today. When texting starts, we will text only people who agreed to it: by checking the text-message box on one of our forms, by texting us first, or as a pilot customer about their own service. We never text a number that did not opt in, and we never buy or rent phone lists.

Messages are about your order, your pilot line or a question you asked. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop at any time, and HELP for help. Consent to texts is never a condition of buying anything.

We do not share or sell mobile numbers or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Numbers are shared only with the texting provider that delivers our messages.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not put ad trackers or cookies for tracking on this website.
  • We share information only with the companies that run our service, like Stripe and our phone and email providers, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we have about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Call (747) 301-8586 and leave your name, the email you used and your request. Do not share card details.

Monthly plan customers can see invoices, update a card or cancel in My account.
