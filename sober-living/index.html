The Problem We Solve

Vacant commercial buildings in North Hollywood and the San Fernando Valley keep costing owners money every month — taxes, insurance, maintenance, and zero return. We turn that liability into a stable, community-serving asset.

The Partnership

  • Master lease: $3,500/mo net via ACH on the 1st, guaranteed even if vacant.
  • Rehab: $10,000 upfront at TTI cost. You keep 100% of improvements.
  • We handle everything: repairs, tenants, compliance, code, insurance ($5M umbrella naming you additional insured).
  • Owner-finance option: 5% interest, 30-year amortization, 5-year balloon — passive income, no realtor fees.
  • Tax benefit: you retain depreciation until sale.
  • Exit: refinance via SBA 7(a) or CDFI once Medi-Cal credentialed and cash-flowing.

Why This Works

Housing-only model

California DHCS does not license sober living / alcohol-and-drug-free housing that provides no treatment services. We operate as housing + peer support + referrals only.

Local compliance first

Every property gets City Planning, LADBS, and LAFD review before any commitment. No binding deal until zoning and use are confirmed.

Community value

6 residents × $35K saved vs incarceration/hospitalization = $210K public value per building per year. 8.4x ROI on a $25K pilot.
