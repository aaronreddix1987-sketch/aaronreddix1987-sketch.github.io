Proposed terms

How we work

Compliance comes first

This is housing only. We offer a sober living environment with peer support and referrals. We do not provide treatment services and do not present ourselves as a licensed treatment provider.

Before any commitment, each property goes through zoning, building and fire review with the city. If the use is not allowed, there is no deal.
