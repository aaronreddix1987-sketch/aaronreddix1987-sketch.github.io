The operating system residents and owners can see on paper.

This page is the public version of the 10X material: action, systems, identity, and tools. It is education and house structure. It is not addiction treatment, not a licensed program, and not a statement that any grant was awarded.

1. Massive action

Idle time is expensive in a house and in a business. The written habit is high-volume useful work: outreach, skill practice, and a schedule that does not leave the day empty.

2. Systems

A house needs repeatable rules the same way a shop needs a close-out checklist. Quiet hours, guests, chores, rent day, and who the manager is. Residents who want a “Sobriety Operating System” use it as personal structure — tracking, mentors they already have, and a pivot if they slip. That work is theirs. It is not delivered as treatment on the property.

3. CEO identity

The books teach money habits, sales practice, and seeing yourself as the operator of your own day. Past mistakes are not sold as a guaranteed future income.

4. AI as a force multiplier

The paid Front Office product is for business owners: first response, lead capture, booking or product-link routing, follow-up. Same idea inside the house: use tools so the manager is not answering the same question twenty times. Disputes, medical, legal, emergency, and account access stay human.
