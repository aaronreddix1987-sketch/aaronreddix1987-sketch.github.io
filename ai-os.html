02 The AI Replacement Ladder

At every rung: tokens first, hires later. Build this in order.

1

Admin — The Gatekeeper

AI triages every email, drafts replies in Aaron's voice, routes what can be delegated, surfaces only what needs a human decision. Currently operational via Claude + Gmail API. Zero inbox management time required.

2

Delivery — The Concierge (Vapi)

AI phone agent answers every client call 24/7. Qualifies callers, books appointments, logs to HubSpot, sends follow-up email — all automatically. Client never waits on hold. No staff required at this rung.

3

Marketing — The Storyteller

Aaron records one 5-minute Loom per week. AI converts it into 12 content pieces: LinkedIn post, email newsletter, YouTube script, cold outreach hook, ebook chapter, case study, sales objection answer, grant narrative, and more.

4

Sales — The Deal Maker

50 AI-drafted cold emails per day → 3-touch follow-up sequence → demo call → AI-generated proposal → Stripe link. Close rate target: 2% cold-to-client. 4 new clients/month at $1,000 MRR average = $4,000 new MRR every month.

5

Leadership — The Digital Brain

Notion database holding all client playbooks, legal status, revenue dashboard, housing tracker, and content library. Any team member — human or AI — queries the brain instead of Aaron. Aaron stops being the bottleneck.

03 The Live Automation Workflow

This stack is operational now — no new tools or spend required.

TRIGGER: New lead calls or emails TTI
STEP 1: Vapi agent answers, qualifies, books appointment → logs to HubSpot
STEP 2: Make.com extracts call data → sends to Claude API
STEP 3: Claude generates personalized follow-up email in Aaron's voice
STEP 4: Gmail sends automatically. HubSpot logs interaction.
STEP 5: If no reply in 48h → automated follow-up sequence fires
STEP 6: Demo booked → AI generates custom proposal → Stripe link sent
STEP 7: Payment received → onboarding sequence launches automatically
AARON'S ROLE: Reviews demo, answers questions, signs the deal. 8% of the work.
